Meet Mr. Hoff.

Dean Hoffman has run his mom-and-pop detail shop from the same spot on Waller Road for nearly four decades. The name on the sign is the man doing the work.

The shop

A mom-and-pop shop, on purpose.

Mr. Hoff’s Auto Detailing is a full-service detail shop in Richmond, Virginia, owned and operated by Dean Hoffman. It has served Virginia drivers from the same location at 4843 Waller Road for almost 40 years.

The shop specializes in ceramic coatings, and handles everything from full interior and exterior details to restoration projects on classic and collector cars. Dean often works on brand-new models, too.

For nearly four decades he has kept prices low and relied on word-of-mouth referrals. That approach earned the shop the top spot for Best Car Detailer in a local reader poll, and a 5.0 rating on Google from 81 reviews. The shop is licensed and insured.
