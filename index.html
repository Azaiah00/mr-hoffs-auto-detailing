Ceramic coating, explained

Water hits it. Water leaves.

A ceramic coating is a liquid layer applied by hand that cures into a hard, slick, high-gloss shell over your clear coat. It’s what Mr. Hoff’s specializes in.

  1. 1

    Wash & decontaminate

    The paint is washed and cleaned of bonded grime, so the coating bonds to paint, not dirt.

  2. 2

    Prep the paint

    Polishing refines the surface. A coating locks in whatever is underneath, so the prep matters most.

  3. 3

    Apply & cure

    The coating goes on panel by panel and cures into a durable, glossy layer.

  4. 4

    Easier care

    Water beads and sheets away, washes go faster, and the shine is easier to keep.

How long a coating lasts depends on the product and how the vehicle is kept. Ask Dean which option fits yours.
