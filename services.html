Ceramic coating

Ceramic coatings are what Mr. Hoff’s specializes in. A coating is a liquid protective layer applied over your clear coat that cures into a hard, slick, high-gloss finish.

The prep: paint prep for ceramic coating

  • Wash and decontamination, so the coating bonds to clean paint
  • Polishing to refine the surface before it is sealed in
  • Coating applied panel by panel, then left to cure

What you notice after

  • Deep gloss that makes the color pop
  • Water beads up and rolls off
  • Dirt releases more easily, so washing is faster

Straight answers

Ceramic & service questions.

Direct answers about coatings, prep and booking.

What is a ceramic coating?

A ceramic coating is a liquid protective layer applied over your vehicle’s clear coat that cures into a hard, slick, glossy finish. Water beads and rolls off, and dirt releases more easily when you wash.

Does my paint need to be prepped before a ceramic coating?

Yes. A coating seals in whatever is underneath it, so the paint is cleaned, decontaminated and polished first. That prep is a big part of how the finished car looks.

How do I wash a ceramic-coated car?

Hand wash with a gentle car shampoo, rinse well and dry with clean microfiber towels. Avoid harsh brush-style automatic washes, and ask Dean for care advice specific to your coating.

How long does a ceramic coating last?

It depends on the coating chosen and how the vehicle is driven, stored and washed. Ask Dean which option fits your car and how to get the most out of it.

Can I book just an interior or just an exterior detail?

Yes. Interior and exterior detailing are available on their own or together as a full detail. Call for a quote on the service you want.

Do you post prices online?

No. Every job is quoted individually because every vehicle is different. Call (804) 355-4633 for a quote.
